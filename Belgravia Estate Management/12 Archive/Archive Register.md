## 1. Purpose

This folder holds records that are no longer live but must be retained: superseded document versions, completed logs, closed projects and historical registers. This register indexes what is archived, when and why.

## 2. Retention guidance

| Record type | Retain for |
|---|---|
| Superseded document versions | Until 2 later versions exist, then review |
| Daily Operations Logs | 12 months live, then archive; keep 3 years |
| Visitor & Delivery Registers | 12 months live, then archive; keep 3 years |
| Incident Reports | Keep minimum 6 years (longer if injury) |
| Compliance certificates | Keep at least until 2 renewals have passed |
| Completed project files | Keep for the life of the asset/works |
| Financial / procurement records | Keep 6 years (tax) |

*These are practical defaults; confirm any statutory requirement with the Family Office.*

## 4. How to archive

1. Move the file into this folder (organise by year/type as needed).
2. Add a line to Section 3 above.
3. Update the Document Control Register (BEM-00-02) status to *Superseded* or *Archived*.
4. Never delete a record before its retention period ends.
