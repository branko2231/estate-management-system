## 4. Programme dependencies

IT Modernisation (CP-01) and Networking (CP-04) are foundational and should be delivered first, as smart-home (CP-02), touchscreen (CP-03), security (CP-05) and audio (CP-06) all depend on them. The building-services trio — HVAC (CP-07), Plumbing (CP-08) and Kitchen (CP-09) — should be coordinated to avoid repeated disruption.
