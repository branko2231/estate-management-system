## 1. Purpose

To return the residence from Family-in-residence mode to secure, well-kept maintenance mode, ensuring nothing is left undone and everything is ready for the long unoccupied period.

## 3. Day of departure

- [ ] Luggage prepared, packed as required and dispatched
- [ ] Vehicles ready and positioned for departure
- [ ] Final requests actioned
- [ ] Any items to travel with the Family confirmed and packed
- [ ] Farewell arrangements handled
- [ ] Confirm forwarding of any post/items with Family Office

## 4. Immediately after departure

- [ ] Full walk-round; note condition and any damage/maintenance
- [ ] Collect and process all laundry and linens
- [ ] Strip and refresh beds
- [ ] Clear and clean bathrooms
- [ ] Kitchen close-down; perishables removed/donated appropriately (Kitchen Inventory)
- [ ] Chef stood down; provisioning reconciled
- [ ] Refuse and recycling cleared

## 5. Within 48 hours

- [ ] Deep clean of used areas (Housekeeping Checklists)
- [ ] Laundry completed, pressed and stored; linens restocked
- [ ] Inventory check of Family areas; log any losses/damage
- [ ] Log all maintenance issues to the Maintenance Register
- [ ] Vehicles cleaned; Corniche returned to storage regime if seasonal
- [ ] Restock consumables used during the stay (Procurement Tracker)

## 6. Return to maintenance mode

- [ ] Climate reset to protective settings (art/antiques)
- [ ] Lighting set to occupancy-simulation / away mode
- [ ] Security fully armed and verified
- [ ] Water flushing routine resumed (legionella)
- [ ] House secured; final check of doors, windows, gates
- [ ] Update Preventative Maintenance Schedule with anything arising
