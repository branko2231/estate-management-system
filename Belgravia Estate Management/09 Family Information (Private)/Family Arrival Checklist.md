## 1. Purpose

The residence must transition from maintenance mode to full Family-in-residence standard seamlessly. This checklist ensures nothing is missed ahead of an arrival. Begin the timeline as soon as dates are confirmed.

## 3. Two weeks before

- [ ] Confirm dates, party size and any guests with Family Office
- [ ] Activate Chef; agree menus and dietary requirements (incl. Indian cuisine)
- [ ] Plan provisioning and place orders (Procurement Tracker)
- [ ] Schedule pre-arrival deep clean (Housekeeping Checklists)
- [ ] Book any contractor works to complete before arrival
- [ ] Confirm vehicles serviced, valeted and ready (Vehicle Register)
- [ ] Review preferences and prior-stay notes

## 4. One week before

- [ ] Deep clean underway across all Family areas
- [ ] Linens and towels laundered, pressed and set (Laundry Procedures)
- [ ] Wardrobes and dressing areas prepared
- [ ] Fresh flowers ordered for arrival day
- [ ] Test all systems: heating, cooling, hot water, AV, network, security
- [ ] Confirm staff rota for the stay
- [ ] Stock pantry staples; schedule perishables for arrival day

## 5. Day before

- [ ] Final deep-clean touches; whole-house presentation walk-round
- [ ] Beds made to standard; rooms set to preference
- [ ] Climate set to comfort in occupied areas
- [ ] Bathrooms replenished (toiletries, towels)
- [ ] Perishables and provisions received and stored
- [ ] Vehicles fuelled/charged, cleaned and positioned
- [ ] Welcome arrangements prepared

## 6. Arrival day

- [ ] Fresh flowers placed
- [ ] Final walk-round to Family-in-residence standard
- [ ] Chef briefed and kitchen ready
- [ ] Welcome refreshments prepared
- [ ] Luggage plan and staff positioned
- [ ] Security and access set for arrival
- [ ] Temperature, lighting and music set to preference

## 7. On arrival

- [ ] Greet and settle the Family
- [ ] Luggage handled and unpacked as required
- [ ] Confirm plans for the day / dinner with Chef
- [ ] Note any immediate requests
