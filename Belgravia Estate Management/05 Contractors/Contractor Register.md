## 1. Purpose

Only vetted, approved contractors work at the residence. This register records their trade, credentials and compliance status. No contractor starts work until insurance and (where relevant) method statements are on file, and no contractor is admitted unannounced. The working data file is the Contractor Database (BEM-05-04, CSV).

## 2. Approval requirements

Before approval, confirm and file:

- [ ] Public liability insurance (min. cover confirmed)
- [ ] Relevant trade certification / registration (e.g. Gas Safe, NICEIC)
- [ ] References checked
- [ ] Confidentiality expectations communicated
- [ ] Risk assessment / method statement (RAMS) for the work
- [ ] Key/access and escort arrangements agreed
