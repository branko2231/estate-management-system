## 1. Purpose

This register controls the lifecycle of every document: who owns it, its current version, when it was last updated and when it is next due for review. No document is considered live until it appears here.

## 2. Control rules

- Every new document is assigned the next document number in its folder series (BEM-XX-NN) and entered below.
- Version numbering: `1.0` at creation; increment the minor number for edits (`1.1`), the major number for a substantive rewrite (`2.0`).
- Review cycle: every document is reviewed at least every six months.
- Superseded versions are moved to `12 Archive` and marked *Superseded* below.
