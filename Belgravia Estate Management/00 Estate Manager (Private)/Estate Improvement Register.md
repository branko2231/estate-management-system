## 1. Purpose

This register captures every idea, observation or opportunity to improve the residence, the service delivered to the Family, or the way the estate is run. Improvements that require capital are promoted to the Capital Projects Register (BEM-04-01); smaller improvements are actioned directly.

## 2. How to use

- Anyone on the team may propose an improvement; the Estate Manager records it here.
- Each item is rated for **Impact** (High/Medium/Low) and **Effort** (High/Medium/Low).
- The Estate Manager reviews the register monthly and sets status.
