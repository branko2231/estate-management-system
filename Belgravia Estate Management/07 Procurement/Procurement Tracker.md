## 1. Purpose

To manage the purchase of goods and services from request through approval, order, delivery and payment. This is the document version; the live data file is the Procurement Tracker CSV (BEM-07-03).

## 2. Procurement rules

- Purchase only from the Approved Supplier Register (BEM-05-03) unless the Estate Manager approves an exception.
- Obtain the appropriate number of quotes by value band (see Section 3).
- All spend is recorded and matched to a delivery (Delivery Register) and an invoice.
- Approval authority rests with the Estate Manager; capital items follow the Capital Projects process.

## 3. Approval thresholds

| Value band | Requirement | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Under £250 | Single supplier; record | Chief Butler / Estate Manager |
| £250 – £2,500 | One–two quotes | Estate Manager |
| £2,500 – £10,000 | Two–three quotes | Estate Manager |
| Over £10,000 | Formal quotes; capital process if applicable | Estate Manager / Family Office |
