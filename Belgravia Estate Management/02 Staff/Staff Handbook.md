## 1. Welcome

Welcome to the household team of this private residence in Belgravia. We serve a family of the highest standing, and our work is defined by discretion, precision and quiet excellence. This handbook sets out how we work together.

## 2. The household team

## 3. Core standards of conduct

1. **Discretion & confidentiality.** Nothing about the Family, guests, property, movements or affairs is ever discussed, photographed, recorded or shared. This obligation continues after employment ends.
2. **Presentation.** Uniform clean and correct; personal grooming immaculate (see Uniform Register).
3. **Punctuality & reliability.** Arrive ready to work; cover is arranged in advance.
4. **Anticipation.** Notice and resolve before being asked.
5. **Professionalism.** Courteous, composed and unobtrusive at all times.

## 4. Confidentiality & data

- Never disclose the address, the Family's identity, movements or schedule.
- No social media references to the household, the property or the Family.
- No photography inside or of the property without Estate Manager authority.
- Report any approach from third parties (press, public, would-be intruders) to the Estate Manager immediately.

## 5. Hours, cover & the 60-day pattern

The Family is in residence approximately 60 days per year; the house operates year-round. Duties, rotas and intensity change between *in-residence* and *maintenance* periods. The Estate Manager publishes the rota; staff must be flexible around Family stays, which may include evenings and weekends.

## 6. Security

- Follow the Standard Operating Procedures Manual (BEM-01-04) for opening, closing and alarm handling.
- Never share alarm codes or keys.
- Challenge and log every visitor (Visitor Register).
- Report anything unusual immediately.

## 7. Health & safety

- Know the Emergency Contact Directory (BEM-10-01) and evacuation routes.
- Report hazards and near-misses; complete an Incident Report Form (BEM-10-02) for any incident.
- Use equipment and chemicals per instructions and COSHH guidance.

## 8. Uniform & appearance

Uniform is issued and recorded in the Uniform Register (BEM-02-03). Uniform is worn correctly on duty and kept immaculate. Report damage or wear for replacement.

## 9. Conduct matters

- Alcohol and illegal substances are prohibited on duty.
- Gifts or gratuities from third parties (e.g. contractors) must be declared to the Estate Manager.
- Personal visitors are not permitted on site without Estate Manager approval.
- Grievances and concerns are raised with the Estate Manager in confidence.

## 10. Communication

- Daily brief from the duty lead.
- Weekly operations meeting (minutes recorded — BEM-04-03).
- Handover notes recorded in the Daily Operations Log.
