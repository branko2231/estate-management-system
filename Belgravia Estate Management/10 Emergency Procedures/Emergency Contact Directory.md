## 7. First-response procedures (summary)

**Fire:** raise alarm → evacuate → 999 → assemble at agreed point → roll-call using Visitor Register → do not re-enter.

**Flood / leak:** isolate water at stopcock → protect valuables/art → call emergency plumber → log incident.

**Power failure:** check with neighbours / call 105 → safe lighting → protect refrigerated/critical items → restore or escalate.

**Gas smell:** do not use switches/flames → ventilate → isolate gas → evacuate → call 0800 111 999.

**Intruder / security:** if in progress, 999 → keep safe → do not confront → preserve CCTV → notify Estate Manager.

**Medical:** 999 → first aid within competence → clear access for ambulance → notify Estate Manager.

## 8. After any incident

Complete an Incident Report Form (BEM-10-02) as soon as it is safe to do so.
