## 1. Purpose

To maintain an accurate inventory of the contents of the residence for care, insurance and audit. High-value items (fine art, antiques, jewellery, collectibles) are also recorded in the Asset Register (BEM-08-01, restricted). The working data file is the Inventory Database (BEM-03-11, CSV).

## 2. Conventions

- Reference format: `INV-[room ref]-NNN`.
- **Value band:** A (£10k+) · B (£1k–£10k) · C (under £1k).
- Photograph high-value items; store references securely.
