## 1. Purpose

To record every fault, defect or maintenance issue from the moment it is reported to the moment it is verified fixed. This is the document version of the register; the CSV tracker (BEM-03-10, Maintenance Tracker) is the working data file.

## 2. Conventions

- **Severity:** P1 (safety / urgent) · P2 (affects function) · P3 (minor) · P4 (cosmetic).
- **Status:** Open · Assigned · In Progress · Awaiting Parts · Complete · Verified.
- Reference format: `M-YYYY-NNN`.
