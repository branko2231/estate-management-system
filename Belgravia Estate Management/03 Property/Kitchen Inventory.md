## 6. Chef activation checklist (pre-stay)

- [ ] Confirm menu plan and dietary requirements with Estate Manager
- [ ] Provisions ordered against menu
- [ ] Fridges/freezers cleaned and at temperature
- [ ] Equipment tested and serviceable
- [ ] Tableware and glassware polished and counted
- [ ] Perishables timed to arrival

## 7. Post-stay close-down

- [ ] Perishables removed/donated appropriately
- [ ] Appliances cleaned and switched to standby
- [ ] Inventory counts updated
- [ ] Reorder list prepared

*Provisioning is planned around Family stays. Staple par-stock is held year-round; perishables are managed to arrival and departure dates.*
