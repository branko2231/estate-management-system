## 1. Standard

The house is kept to Family-in-residence presentation standard at all times. Work quietly and methodically; protect finishes, art and antiques; report anything found damaged or out of place. Follow Room Information Sheets (BEM-03-03) for room-specific methods and cautions.

## 2. Daily checklist (occupied)

- [ ] Air and refresh occupied rooms
- [ ] Beds made / turned down as required
- [ ] Bathrooms cleaned and replenished
- [ ] Floors cleaned appropriate to finish
- [ ] Surfaces dusted; glass and mirrors clear
- [ ] Kitchen and service areas cleaned
- [ ] Refuse and recycling removed
- [ ] Fresh flowers checked / water changed
- [ ] Rooms reset to presentation standard

## 3. Daily checklist (unoccupied maintenance)

- [ ] Walk-round; confirm no damp, leaks, pests or damage
- [ ] Dust principal rooms on rota
- [ ] Run taps / flush WCs (legionella prevention)
- [ ] Check climate settings for art/antique protection
- [ ] Confirm security and lighting appearance

## 4. Weekly checklist

- [ ] Full vacuum including edges and under furniture
- [ ] Skirting, ledges and high dusting
- [ ] Mirrors and internal glass polished
- [ ] Bathrooms deep-wiped; limescale treated
- [ ] Kitchen appliances wiped down
- [ ] Bin areas cleaned
- [ ] Linens laundered / rotated (see Laundry Procedures)

## 5. Monthly checklist

- [ ] Upholstery and soft furnishings cared for
- [ ] Light fittings and shades dusted
- [ ] Interior windows cleaned
- [ ] Silver / metalware checked
- [ ] Wardrobes and storage aired
- [ ] Skirting and cornice detailed

- [ ] Carpets / rugs specialist-cleaned as needed

## 7. Product & method notes

| Surface | Approved product | Method | Do not use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marble / stone | pH-neutral cleaner | Soft cloth, no acids | Vinegar, bleach |
| Antique wood | Specialist wax/polish | Soft cloth | Silicone sprays |
| Silver | Silver cloth / dip | Gentle | Abrasives |
| Fine art | Do not clean | Report to Estate Manager | Any product |
