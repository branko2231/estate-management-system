## 5. Access, keys & security

- Keys and access devices are controlled by the Estate Manager and issued individually.
- Alarm codes are personal and never shared.
- The full access schedule is held in the Property Information Register (restricted).

## 6. Daily operating notes

- Opening and closing follow SOP-01 and SOP-02 (Standard Operating Procedures Manual).
- Presentation standard is maintained year-round, not only during stays.
- Environmental settings (temperature, humidity) are held to protect finishes, art and instruments even when unoccupied.

## 7. Contractors & maintenance

- Approved contractors only (Contractor Register, BEM-05-01).
- Faults are logged in the Maintenance Register (BEM-03-04).
- Planned servicing follows the Preventative Maintenance Schedule (BEM-03-05).

## 8. Emergencies

Refer to the Emergency Contact Directory (BEM-10-01) and the Emergency Procedures folder. Key isolation points are listed in Section 3 above — ensure all duty staff know them.

## 9. Room Information Sheets

Each room has its own sheet (BEM-03-03) covering finishes, contents, cleaning notes and special instructions. Keep them current as the house changes.
