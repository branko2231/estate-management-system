## 1. Standard

Laundry is handled to couture-care standard: garments and linens are protected, correctly processed and returned immaculate. When in doubt about a fabric or garment, consult the care label and the Estate Manager; delicate or couture items go to the approved specialist.

## 2. Sorting

- Separate by: colour, fabric type, temperature, and delicacy.
- Check pockets; remove and safeguard any items found (log valuables).
- Pre-treat stains promptly; record any garment at risk.
- Keep Family items entirely separate from staff items.

## 3. Washing & drying reference

| Fabric / item | Wash | Dry | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bed linen (cotton/percale) | 40–60°C | Line/tumble low, then press | Iron damp for finish |
| Towels | 40–60°C | Tumble | No fabric softener on some finishes |
| Fine cottons / shirts | 30–40°C | Hang, press | Hand-finish collars/cuffs |
| Silk / delicates | Hand / cold delicate | Flat / hang | Never wring |
| Wool / cashmere | Hand / wool cycle cold | Flat dry | Reshape while damp |
| Couture / tailoring | Specialist only | — | Do not process in-house |

## 4. Pressing & finishing

- Press to the correct standard for each item; hang or fold as appropriate.
- Shirts finished on hangers; bed linen pressed and stored in sets.
- Steam delicate items rather than direct-press where required.
