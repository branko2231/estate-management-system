## 1. Purpose

Every person entering the residence who is not a member of staff is recorded here for security, safety (evacuation roll-call) and audit. No unannounced access is permitted.

## 2. Rules

- Confirm the appointment before arrival.
- Verify identity on arrival.
- Escort visitors at all times in private areas.
- Sign every visitor out on departure.
