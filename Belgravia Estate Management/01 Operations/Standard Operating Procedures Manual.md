## 1. Purpose & scope

This manual defines *how things are done* at the residence so that standards are consistent regardless of who is on duty. Each SOP is short, actionable and owned. Where a task has its own detailed checklist elsewhere (e.g. housekeeping, arrival/departure), this manual points to it.

## 2. Standard of service — guiding principles

1. **Discretion first.** Nothing about the Family, the property or the household is discussed outside the team.
2. **Presentation-ready always.** The house is kept to Family-in-residence standard year-round.
3. **Anticipate, don't react.** Problems are found on the daily walk-round, not by the Family.
4. **Record everything.** If it wasn't logged, it didn't happen.
5. **Quiet excellence.** Service is seamless, unobtrusive and precise.

## 4. SOP-01 — Opening the house (daily)

1. Disarm alarm using personal code; confirm no overnight events on the panel/CCTV.
2. Complete external then internal walk-round per the Daily Operations Log.
3. Confirm heating/cooling/hot water; note any fault.
4. Collect and log post/deliveries.
5. Confirm staff attendance and brief the day.

## 5. SOP-02 — Closing / securing the house (daily)

1. Confirm all faults logged.
2. Check all doors, windows and gates secured.
3. Set lighting to occupancy/away mode.
4. Close down kitchen.
5. Arm the alarm; confirm set.
6. Close and sign the Daily Operations Log.

## 6. SOP-03 — Security & alarm handling

- On alarm activation, follow the escalation in the Emergency Contact Directory (BEM-10-01).
- Never disclose alarm codes. Codes are held by the Estate Manager and issued individually.
- CCTV footage is confidential; release only on Estate Manager authority.

## 7. SOP-04 — Receiving deliveries

1. Verify expected delivery against the Weekly Planner / Procurement Tracker.
2. Inspect packaging before signing; note damage.
3. Record in the Delivery Register (BEM-01-06).
4. Store perishables immediately; route items to the correct department.

## 8. SOP-05 — Receiving visitors & contractors

1. Confirm the appointment in advance; no unannounced access.
2. Verify identity; record in the Visitor Register (BEM-01-05).
3. Issue a visitor badge if applicable; escort at all times in private areas.
4. Contractors: confirm insurance/RAMS on file (Contractor Register) before work starts.
5. Sign visitor out on departure.

## 9. SOP-06 — Logging a maintenance fault

1. Record the fault in the Maintenance Register (BEM-03-04) with location, severity and date.
2. For P1 safety issues, isolate the hazard and notify the Estate Manager immediately.
3. Raise to the approved contractor if beyond in-house capability.
4. Close the fault only when verified fixed.
