## 1. Purpose

To track every delivery, confirm goods against orders, flag damage before signing, and route items to the correct department.

## 2. Rules

- Verify against the Weekly Planner / Procurement Tracker where an order exists.
- Inspect packaging before signing; photograph and note any damage.
- Store perishables and valuables immediately.
- Log all high-value items to the relevant inventory/asset register.
